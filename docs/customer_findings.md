# Customer Segment Findings

## Definitions

Customer Key:

customer_unique_id

New Customer:

First observed delivered purchase month
equals the current analysis month.

Existing Customer:

Customer with a delivered purchase
after the first observed purchase month.

90-day Repeat:

A new customer with another delivered order
1 to 90 days after the first observed purchase.

## Limitations

- First Purchase means first observed purchase in the available Olist data.
- Purchase history before the dataset start is unavailable.
- Only delivered orders are used.
- Customers without a complete 90-day observation window are excluded.
- Repeat rate does not establish the effect of CRM or marketing activities.
