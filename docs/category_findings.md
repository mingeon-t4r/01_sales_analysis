## Limitations

- Item Sales is not accounting Net Sales.
- Product cost and margin are unavailable.
- Discount and refund amounts are unavailable.
- A single order or customer may appear in multiple product categories.
- Category-level Order Count and Buyer Count must not be summed to reproduce overall totals.
- Observed category changes do not establish causal effects.
